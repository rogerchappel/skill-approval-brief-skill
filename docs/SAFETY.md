# Safety Checklist

- Confirm the target system and action are named in the approval text. Validation requires each complete phrase after case folding and normalization of punctuation and whitespace; partial words and abbreviations do not match.
- Confirm rollback is realistic before asking for approval.
- Treat `forbidden` as blocked, not as a stronger approval prompt.
- Keep payload previews short and redacted.
- Preserve the generated brief with the run audit trail.
